## Method, source health & caveats
- **Primary screen — TradingView public screener, HTTP 200.** Two pulls against `POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`: `premarket_change` in [3,1000] sorted desc (160 names) and [-1000,-3] sorted asc (321 names). - **ntrt backend — failed.** `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused), so this is Mode B (screener fallback). No MCP mode was attempted.
- **Bridge `market-recap` — ok, 2 calls.** `market_snapshot` and `get_quotes` returned HTTP 200 for the market backdrop and for previous-close verification only (SPY 766.81, QQQ 691.95, and APP's 271.75 Nov-4 close all matched the screener-derived values exactly).
- **MCP probe — `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`**, bridge reachable with packs tradier, fmp, market-recap. No finance MCP is registered on this runtime, so the public screener was used, per the skill.
- **Catalysts — `web_search`** for all 69 actionable names, cross-checked against company releases (Business Wire / GlobeNewswire / StockTitan), AP, Reuters and investing.com. One catalyst could not be confirmed (TE) and is flagged rather than filled in.
- **Previous close is derived, not quoted.** TradingView's `close` column is the LIVE intraday price once the session is open (SPY `close` 754.64 vs the Nov-4 close 766.81), so it cannot be used as the prior close. Prior close was derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change)` and validated against the bridge for three names — exact match on all three.
- **Yahoo pre-market was not used for any published price.** Yahoo reports 0 volume on every pre-market bar and its tape goes stale on thin names; pre-market volume here is TradingView's own `premarket_volume`.
- **Denominator caveat:** `average_volume_30d_calc` is a 30-session DAILY average, so a %ADV above 100 means the pre-open session alone traded more than a normal full day. Two names (JAN, HLP) are affected.
- **One pull, one screen.** Every number in this brief and in the CSV comes from the single pre-market pull pair above; the market backdrop quotes the bridge's 15:10Z snapshot and is labelled with its time. Names whose gap crossed the 3% threshold after the pull would not appear here.
