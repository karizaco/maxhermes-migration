# Track A — Structural consistency of metainstructions across the 23 cloud cron routines

**Scope.** `/workspace/mig/cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/*.md` (22 files) plus
`/workspace/mig/cloud-prompts/01-weekly-mcp-health-check.md` (the 23rd, which lives at the
cloud-prompts root). Repo HEAD `24baf07`. Region of interest: the metainstruction/instruction
sections — everything from the start of each file up to (excluding) `### PRIMARY SOURCE: the
MCP-over-HTTP bridge (use this FIRST)` where present, plus everything from `## CRITICAL REMINDERS`
to end of file. The middle body (PRIMARY SOURCE → end of RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT) is out of
scope and was not audited.

**Method.** Every H2/H3 section of all 23 prompts was parsed and indexed; window-flag triples,
skip-marker filenames, skill URLs, artifact paths and the four shared blocks (`## GIT PUSH`,
`## DELIVERY VERIFICATION`, `## DELIVERY`, `## CRITICAL REMINDERS`) were extracted and
canonically normalised (routine slug, output directory, commit message and flag values replaced
with placeholders) before grouping, so that a group of N members means "byte-identical after
removing only the routine's own identity". Differs are reported as unified diffs against the
`#02` baseline. Scripts: `/workspace/.mavis/plans/plan_9bd4fa95/workspace/track-a/`.

**Limitation — the live scheduler was not reachable in this session.** `tool_search` returned no
hits for `cron*`/`schedule`/`task`, and `mcp_invoke cron_read` returned `Unknown tool_name`. The
"live scheduler" column below is therefore sourced from the repo's own scheduler-derived
authorities, all of which are documented as generated from the live scheduler:
`tools/schedule-ground-truth.json` (`_derived_from`: "migration-state.json @ 2026-09-25 + README.md
post-migration changes", `_invariant`: "Every weekday routine is 1-5"), `cron-prompts/roster.json`
(`as_of_et: 2026-10-31`, carries per-routine `task_id`), `cron-prompts/ROSTER.md` ("Generated by
`tools/gen_roster.py` from the live scheduler and `origin/main`. As of 2026-10-31 ET") and
`cron-prompts/README.md` (generated from `tools/schedule-ground-truth.json` + the live scheduler).
The original pre-migration local schedules come from `migration-state.json` (the 2026-09-25
`mavis cron list` snapshot), which `schedule-ground-truth.json` names as its derivation source.

## Summary

The fleet is **structurally consistent at its core and inconsistent at its edges**. The `## GIT
PUSH` prose is one canonical form across all 19 template-shaped prompts, and its 3-attempt
race-retry loop is byte-identical across all 19 (only the `git add` line in `#19` and the
collision comment above it in `#16`/`#26` differ); the `## DELIVERY VERIFICATION` prose is one
canonical form across all 19; the window-check bash block is one canonical form across 16 of the
20 routines that have a fenced one (`#15` adds an hour gate, `#19` a holiday table, `#26` a
hard-coded day test, `#90` an echo-only stub); all 20 skill URLs resolve to a real file; and
every one of the 19 deployed cron expressions the prompts state agrees with
`schedule-ground-truth.json`, `roster.json`, `ROSTER.md` and the `README.md` index. Drift is
concentrated in six places: (1) the `## SCHEDULE` block states a wrong "Original local schedule"
in 4 routines (`#05`, `#20`, `#23`, `#26`) and states no deployed cron expression at all in 4
more (`#06`, `#13`, `#14`, `#19`) — 8 distinct routines; (2) the window-check flag triple
disagrees with the scheduler in exactly one routine (`#16`) and is absent entirely in 3 of the 21
routines that have a window check (`#07`, `#26`, `#90`); (3) the `## MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE` block
advertises a tool (`extract_content_from_websites`) that the shared contract says does not exist,
in 16 of the 17 blocks that carry it; (4) three routines impose reply line caps that the shared
reply-format contract explicitly abolished, and 17 of 19 still define `errors: <n>` as "count of
MCP failures" for a tool that is not registered; (5) 18 of the 21 prompts with a `## GIT PUSH`
block carry a stale push-collision list naming two collisions that no longer exist; (6) `#90`'s
push block builds the audit branch on `origin/main` — the known-bad pattern is **confirmed**. Two
findings are outright defects rather than drift: `#26` points its `## CRITICAL REMINDERS` at a
`## RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT` section that does not exist in its file, and 17 tails promise
holiday skipping that only `#19` implements.

## Findings

### A1 — `## CRITICAL REMINDERS` is missing from two routines and `## RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT` from four

- Severity: high
- Routines affected: `#07` (no CRITICAL REMINDERS), `#01` (no CRITICAL REMINDERS, no SCHEDULE, no WINDOW CHECK, no SKILL SPEC, no MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE, no OUTPUT, no GIT PUSH, no DELIVERY VERIFICATION, no DELIVERY); `#26`, `#90`, `#91` (no RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT)
- Evidence:
  - `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/07-stock-screener-focus-rvol-09:45.md:333` — `## DELIVERY (the only thing delivered to the chat)` is the last section; the file ends at `:341` with no `## CRITICAL REMINDERS`.
  - `cloud-prompts/01-weekly-mcp-health-check.md:19` — `## Cron form fields (paste these into agent.minimax.io)` is the first `##`; the prompt body is nested inside a ` ``` ` fence at `:42`.
  - `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md:75` — `## MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE` runs to `:174`, then `:175` `## ZERO-FALLBACK POLICY`. There is no `## RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT` anywhere in the file (`grep -n 'RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT' 26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md` returns exactly one hit, `:320`, which is the dangling reference in A2).
- Problem: `#07` is the only routine whose tail carries none of the four standing reminders — in particular not "Do NOT call the `mcp__*` tools — they are not registered in this runtime — and do not invent tool names". It relies on the reader of `### PRIMARY SOURCE` instead, which is exactly the trap `RUNTIME-CONTRACT.md:30`–`:32` warns about ("The prompt's own CRITICAL REMINDER originally said 'use the EXACT MCP tool names listed above', which pushed the agent straight into that trap"). `#01` is structurally not a routine prompt at all: it is a form-filling document whose agent instructions live inside a fenced block, so no tooling that walks H2 sections (including this repo's own `tools/gen_index.py` and `tools/gen_roster.py`) can see its schedule, artifact or reply contract.
- Recommended fix: (a) append a `## CRITICAL REMINDERS` section to `07-stock-screener-focus-rvol-09:45.md` after `:341` containing the four standing items (skill-fetch-first, tools-only-from-the-contract, fresh-session, out-of-window exit) plus the `### PRIMARY SOURCE` bridge rule; (b) either promote `#01` to a first-class routine prompt with the standard 13 H2 sections, or add a `## CRITICAL REMINDERS` / `## DELIVERY` pair to it and record in `README.md:119` that `#01` is a form-filling document rather than a routine.
- Confidence: high

### A2 — `#26`'s `## CRITICAL REMINDERS` points at a section that does not exist in its own file

- Severity: high
- Routines affected: `#26`
- Evidence: `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md:320` — "Use ONLY the tools named in the RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT above (`web_search`, `web_fetch`, `bash`). Do NOT call the `mcp__*` tools — they are not / registered in this runtime — and do not invent tool names."
- Problem: there is no `## RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT` section in `#26`. The runtime contract was folded into the 100-line `## MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE` block (`:75`–`:174`), which still advertises `extract_content_from_websites` (`:77`) and then contradicts itself at `:79` ("all `mcp__yahoo_finance__*` / `mcp__tradingview*` tools are NOT registered in this runtime"). The tail's instruction is therefore unresolvable by section name, and the section the agent is actually pointed at (`## MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE`) is the one place in the whole file that tells it to use a tool the shared contract says does not exist.
- Recommended fix: split `26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md` at `:78` so the `## MCP TOOLS AVAILABLE` block keeps only the two provenance lines, and re-open the text from `:79` as `## RUNTIME DATA-SOURCE CONTRACT (read before fetching any data)`, inserting `### PRIMARY SOURCE: the MCP-over-HTTP bridge (use this FIRST)` at the same relative position the other 20 routines use (i.e. immediately before the bridge bash block currently at `:92`). Then `#26` has the standard 14-section order and `:320` resolves.
- Confidence: high

### A3 — `#16` is the only weekday routine whose window check sets `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false`

- Severity: high
- Routines affected: `#16` (the only one of the 16 Mon-Fri routines that gets it wrong)
- Evidence: `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/16-unusual-options-flow-daily.md:24` — `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false`; its scheduler schedule is `35 16 * * 1-5` (`tools/schedule-ground-truth.json`, `cron-prompts/roster.json` → `16-unusual-options-flow-daily`, `cron-prompts/README.md:39`). Compare `02-asia-pre-market-reactions.md:24`, `04:24`, `05:24`, `08:24`, `09:24`, `10:41`, `12:41`, `15:20`, `17:41`, `19:20`, `20:24`, `23:24`, `25:24` — 13 routines, all `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=true`. The remaining Mon-Fri routines are `#07` (`:35`–`:50`, hard-codes `if [ "$DOW" -ge 6 ]`), `#26` (`:43`, same hard-coded test) and `#16`. `06:19`, `13:19`, `14:19`, `22:24` are also `false`, but those are Sat / Wed / Sun / Sun, which is correct.
- Problem: `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false` disables the only guard that would stop a weekend fire. The scheduler currently holds `1-5`, so this is latent — but `tools/schedule-ground-truth.json:_why` records that on 2026-10-31 "the same corruption hit 14 routines in a 05:40-09:08 ET sweep", collapsing `1-5` to `1`. The corruption is silent because `next_run` does not change. If `#16`'s `1-5` is ever widened to `*`, it fires at 16:35 on Saturday and Sunday, its `MARKET_HOURS_ONLY=true` gate passes (hour 16 ∈ [9,17)), and it writes a Saturday `options-flow/<date>.md` that no other routine can explain.
- Recommended fix: in `16-unusual-options-flow-daily.md:24`, replace `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false` with `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=true`.
- Confidence: high

### A4 — `#26` drops the flag triple entirely, so its window check cannot be parsed by the same convention as the other 18 of the 21 routines that have one

- Severity: medium
- Routines affected: `#26` (and `#07`, which is bespoke)
- Evidence: `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md:38`–`:47` — the block contains no `WEEKDAYS_ONLY` / `WEEKEND_ONLY` / `MARKET_HOURS_ONLY` / `DAY_OF_WEEK` assignments; the day test is hard-coded: `if [ "$DOW" -ge 6 ]; then SKIP_REASON="weekend (day=$DOW, expected Mon-Fri)"; fi`. Compare `02-asia-pre-market-reactions.md:24`–`:27`, which assigns all four.
- Problem: the function is equivalent to `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=true`, but the *form* is gone. A drift detector that reads the flags out of the prompt (the natural way to cross-check the prompt against the scheduler) sees no flags at all and cannot tell whether `#26` is weekday-only, weekend-only or unconstrained. `#26` is also the only template-shaped prompt whose `## SCHEDULE` says `Mon-Fri only` while its window-check code cannot express that constraint.
- Recommended fix: in `26-agentic-pre-market-brief.md:38`, insert the four standard assignments and replace the hard-coded `if [ "$DOW" -ge 6 ]` with the standard two-clause form:
  ```
  WEEKDAYS_ONLY=true
  WEEKEND_ONLY=false
  MARKET_HOURS_ONLY=false
  DAY_OF_WEEK=0   # 1=Mon ... 7=Sun; 0=no constraint
  ```
  followed by `if [ "$WEEKDAYS_ONLY" = "true" ] && [ "$DOW" -ge 6 ]; then SKIP_REASON="weekend (day=$DOW, expected Mon-Fri)"; fi`.
- Confidence: high

### A5 — `#06` uses `WEEKEND_ONLY=true` + `DAY_OF_WEEK=0` while the two Sunday routines use `DAY_OF_WEEK=7`

- Severity: medium
- Routines affected: `#06`, versus `#14`, `#22`
- Evidence: `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/06-stock-screener-pattern-study-backlog.md:19`–`:22` — `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false` / `WEEKEND_ONLY=true` / `MARKET_HOURS_ONLY=false` / `DAY_OF_WEEK=0`; scheduler `0 15 * * 6` (Saturday). `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/14-macro-calendar-week-ahead.md:19`–`:22` and `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/22-weekly-market-recap-preview.md:24`–`:27` — both `DAY_OF_WEEK=7`.
- Problem: the two non-weekday weekly routines model "Sunday" with an exact day constraint, while the Saturday routine models "weekend" with a loose one that also accepts Sunday. If `#06`'s scheduler expression is ever widened from `6` to `*`, it fires on Sunday: `WEEKDAYS_ONLY=false` does not block it, `WEEKEND_ONLY=true` does not block it (DOW=7 ≥ 6), and `DAY_OF_WEEK=0` imposes nothing — so `#06` would publish a Saturday-only "weekly-ipo" artifact on a Sunday. The three weekly routines should use one model.
- Recommended fix: in `06-stock-screener-pattern-study-backlog.md:19`–`:22`, set `WEEKEND_ONLY=false` and `DAY_OF_WEEK=6` (Saturday, `%u` convention), matching the `#14`/`#22` model.
- Confidence: high

### A6 — `#06`, `#13` and `#14` state no deployed cron expression, and `#06`'s "Schedule is unchanged" claim is false

- Severity: medium
- Routines affected: `#06`, `#13`, `#14`
- Evidence: `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/06-stock-screener-pattern-study-backlog.md:7`–`:8` — "- Original local schedule: Saturday weekly" / "- Schedule is unchanged — only the runtime environment changed." Same two lines at `13-macro-calendar-midweek.md:7`–`:8` and `14-macro-calendar-week-ahead.md:7`–`:8`. Every other template prompt carries an explicit `- Deployed schedule: \`<cron>\` America/New_York — <time> ET, <days>.` line (e.g. `02-asia-pre-market-reactions.md:8`).
  The claim is contradicted by the repo's own record of the pre-migration schedule: `migration-state.json:65`–`:66` — `"cronName": "Stock-screener pattern-study backlog (Saturday)"`, `"schedule": "0 10 * * 6"` — i.e. Saturday **10:00**, not the deployed `0 15 * * 6` (Saturday 15:00, `cron-prompts/roster.json` → `06-stock-screener-pattern-study-backlog`).
- Problem: these three prompts are the only template-shaped ones whose `## SCHEDULE` cannot be cross-checked against the scheduler at all, because they name no cron expression. `#06`'s prose goes further and asserts the schedule is unchanged when it moved by five hours — the one field in the prompt that a human would use to verify the migration is wrong in exactly the routine that had the largest move among the three.
- Recommended fix: replace the two lines in each of the three files with the standard pair, e.g. for `#06`:
  ```
  - Original local schedule: `0 10 * * 6` America/New_York — Saturday 10:00 ET.
  - Deployed schedule: `0 15 * * 6` America/New_York — Saturday 15:00 ET. Moved from 10:00 ET on 2026-10-29 to clear the 15:00 push slot.
  ```
  and for `#13` (`0 14 * * 3`, Wed 14:00) and `#14` (`0 15 * * 0`, Sun 15:00) the same two-field form with their expressions.
- Confidence: high

### A7 — the "Original local schedule" field is wrong in four routines

- Severity: medium
- Routines affected: `#05`, `#20`, `#23`, `#26`
- Evidence:
  - `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/05-eod-international-daily.md:7` — "- Original local schedule: 16:30 ET weekdays"; the record says `migration-state.json:54`–`:55` — `"cronName": "Daily EOD international pull (EU/ASX/JP/TW/CA)"`, `"schedule": "35 16 * * 1-5"` = **16:35**.
  - `cloud-prompts/cron-prompts/20-eod-watchlist-csv-snapshot.md:7` — "- Original local schedule: 16:00 ET weekdays"; the record says `migration-state.json:97`–`:98` — `"cronName": "Daily EOD watchlist CSV snapshot"`, `"schedule": "30 16 * * 1-5"` = **16:30**.

### A8 — `#19`'s `## SCHEDULE` contradicts itself and drops the standard two-field form
